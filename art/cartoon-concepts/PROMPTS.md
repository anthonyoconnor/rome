# Little Rome — cartoon style concepts

Two generated gameplay concepts exploring a cartoon art direction. These are concept images, not captures or changes to the playable game. Generated using the built-in imagegen tool. Original output files are preserved without post-processing.

- `01-soft-3d-cartoon.png`: rounded, sculpted 3D cartoon architecture and citizens.
- `02-drawn-cartoon.png`: expressive outlined cartoon art with broad cel shading.
